Privacy Policy for Handheld Accountant

Version scope: This revision describes Handheld Accountant 2.0, planned for release on 25 November 2026. Some screens and features differ in older versions. Earlier releases may have shown app-open advertisements or requested tracking permission; existing system permissions remain controlled in your device settings.

Original policy effective date: 25 July 2026
This revision effective date: 24 November 2026
Last updated: 24 November 2026

Gallop & Thrive Limited, operating as Gallop & Thrive (“Gallop & Thrive”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), provides the Handheld Accountant mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, why it is processed, how it is stored and shared, and the choices available to you.

This policy describes the updated App with profile-based receipt organisation, warranty records, and export preparation. It explains our practices; it does not replace any consent required by law or an operating-system permission.

1. Information processed by the App

Profile and receipt information

The App lets you create profiles and record information from receipts. Depending on how you use the App, this information may include:

  • profile names you choose and optional company names; a profile name need not be your legal name;
  • tax labels, tax rates, currency preferences, and other profile settings;
  • receipt images;
  • merchant or trading names and legal entity names;
  • purchase dates, totals, currencies, tax labels, tax rates, estimated tax and net amounts, and payment methods;
  • built-in or custom categories, Business/Personal designations, and notes;
  • receipt numbers, purchase locations, supplier tax ID types and numbers, and associated country codes;
  • optional warranty expiry dates and per-profile reminder preferences;
  • app-generated record, profile, and category identifiers, archive status, and the last selected profile; and
  • edits you make to scanned or imported receipt information.

This information is provided by you or extracted from receipt images using Apple’s on-device text-recognition technologies.

Camera and photo library

With your permission, the App may access:

  • the camera, so you can scan a receipt; and
  • photos that you select from your photo library, so you can import an existing receipt image.

The camera is used when you start a scan and allow camera access. Photo import uses Apple's selection interface to receive images you choose; importing a selected image does not require unrestricted access to your entire photo library. You can manage available permissions in your device Settings.

Warranty notifications

Warranty reminders are optional and off by default for each profile. If you enable them, the App requests notification permission and schedules local notifications using the warranty dates you record. The notification content may include the profile name, merchant name, and recorded expiry date; notification metadata includes the profile and receipt identifiers needed to open the matching record. These reminders are scheduled on your device, not sent from a notification server operated by us.

Notification previews may be visible on your Lock Screen or other notification surfaces, depending on your Apple settings. You can disable reminders for a profile in the App and change notification permission or preview visibility in your device Settings. Warranty records remain usable without notification permission.

Purchase information

If you purchase or restore the Full Version, Apple processes the transaction through the App Store. The App receives information necessary to confirm whether the Full Version entitlement is active. We do not receive or store your full payment-card or banking details.

Advertising and device information

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads for banner advertisements. App-open advertisements are disabled in the updated App covered by this revision. Google and participating advertising partners may process advertising-related information separately from your locally stored receipt records, including:

  • device and advertising identifiers, where permitted;
  • IP address;
  • general device information, operating-system version, language, and approximate location inferred from IP address;
  • advertisement impressions, interactions, clicks, crash diagnostics, and performance information;
  • consent and privacy choices; and
  • information used for fraud prevention, security, frequency capping, advertising measurement, and, where permitted, personalized advertising.

The information processed depends on the SDK and advertising configuration, applicable privacy choices, and device permissions. We do not supply your receipt images, receipt contents, profile names, supplier tax IDs, or notes to Google as advertising targeting information.

2. How information is used

Information is processed to:

  • scan receipts and recognize receipt text;
  • create and manage profiles and receipt records;
  • display, search, filter, edit, categorise, archive, delete, and export receipts;
  • calculate receipt-level estimates and currency-separated summaries, and suggest categories using previous receipts stored on your device;
  • maintain warranty records and schedule optional local reminders;
  • prepare and review selected records and create CSV, financial-summary PDF, or receipt-image PDF exports when requested;
  • provide and restore the Full Version;
  • remove advertising when the Full Version is active;
  • display and measure advertisements in the free version;
  • maintain security, prevent fraud, diagnose errors, and improve reliability; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable terms.

Receipt information is not used by Gallop & Thrive to make financial, accounting, tax, credit, insurance, employment, or other automated eligibility decisions.

3. On-device processing and storage

Receipt text recognition is performed on the device using Apple Vision technologies. Based on the current version of the App, Gallop & Thrive does not upload your receipt images or extracted receipt contents to a Gallop & Thrive server for text recognition.

Profiles, receipt records, saved receipt images, thumbnails, categories, preferences, and cached Full Version status are stored locally using Apple storage technologies. Category suggestions use the existing receipts on your device; they do not require a remote AI service. The App does not currently provide its own cloud receipt database or account-based cloud synchronisation. Apple still verifies purchases and advertising services may communicate over the network as described in this policy. This locally stored information may also be included in device backups depending on your Apple device and backup settings.

If a future version introduces cloud synchronization, remote receipt processing, analytics, or another server-based feature, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when that processing begins.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your receipt images, extracted receipt contents, or profile information.

Information may be shared or processed in the following circumstances:

User-directed sharing

When you prepare an export, the App creates files locally from the records selected in the current profile. Exported information may include profile and company names, merchant and legal entity names, dates, monetary and tax information, categories, Business/Personal status, notes, receipt numbers, purchase locations, supplier tax identifiers, warranty dates, archive status, and record or profile identifiers. CSV exports also include the selection scope and export timestamp. The receipt PDF archive can contain original receipt images, including any personal details visible in those images.

When you save or share a file, the destination you select through Apple's sharing interface determines who receives or stores it. This can include a Files location, email recipient, accountant, third-party app, or cloud storage service. Review the selected records, images, and destination before sharing. The recipient's privacy practices apply. Preparing an export does not automatically send it to us or an accountant.

Archiving a receipt hides it from the active list but does not delete its record or images. Optional archiving after sharing is a separate action; copies already saved or shared are not recalled by archiving or deleting the original.

Google advertising services

Google Mobile Ads and participating advertising partners may process advertising, device, consent, diagnostic, and interaction information as described above. Google's handling of advertising information is described in its own policies, including:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Apple services

Apple processes App Store purchases, restores, TestFlight distribution, operating-system permissions, and related information under Apple’s privacy policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Information you send to support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include. Send receipt images or other personal information only if needed for your request. We use support correspondence to respond, troubleshoot, and handle privacy requests, and retain it only as reasonably needed for those purposes and applicable legal obligations.

Legal and safety reasons

For information that we actually hold, such as support correspondence, we may disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, or a valid governmental request; protect the rights, safety, and security of users or others; investigate fraud or abuse; or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Business changes

If Gallop & Thrive is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, information associated with the App may be transferred subject to appropriate confidentiality and privacy protections.

5. Advertising choices, consent, and tracking

Google's User Messaging Platform manages available advertising consent and privacy messages where required. Ad requests are gated on the consent SDK permitting them. Permission to display an ad is not, by itself, permission for cross-app tracking.

The updated App does not initiate an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission prompt. Any existing system tracking permission remains controlled by iOS or iPadOS. Advertising consent does not override an ATT refusal or restriction; access to Apple's advertising identifier requires the relevant system permission. Advertisements may still be available without that permission. Disabling ATT does not mean all advertising-related network processing stops.

You can review or change Apple tracking permission at:

Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking

Where the App displays a “Privacy and cookie settings” option, you may use it to review available advertising privacy choices. You may also control certain personalized-ad settings through your Apple device or Google account, where available.

When the Full Version entitlement is recognised, the App disables its advertisements. This does not erase information previously processed by advertising providers.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local profile and receipt information remains on your device until you delete individual receipts, delete a profile, or remove the App. Deleting a profile is intended to delete its associated receipt records and locally stored receipt images. Removing the App generally removes its locally stored data, subject to Apple backup and restoration behavior.

Temporary files created for CSV or PDF sharing may remain in the App's temporary storage until cleaned up by the operating system or removed with the App. Deleting a receipt does not necessarily remove an export already created from it. Files saved to Files, email, cloud storage, or receiving apps remain subject to those destinations' retention and deletion controls.

Archiving is not deletion. To remove a saved or shared copy, delete it separately from its destination. Turning off a profile's warranty reminders removes its pending reminders when the App refreshes scheduling; already delivered notifications may need to be cleared through the system interface.

Advertising and purchase providers retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

Because Gallop & Thrive does not currently maintain user accounts or a server-side copy of your receipt database, we cannot remotely retrieve, restore, or delete information stored only on your device. Device backups and previously shared copies may retain information after its removal from the App. CSV and PDF files are useful records, but are not a complete backup that can restore the App's database.

7. Security

We use the protections provided by iOS and iPadOS and take reasonable measures designed to protect information handled by the App. However, no device, storage system, or method of electronic processing is completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining control of your device, passcode, backups, exported files, and destinations to which you share information.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is intended as a general receipt-organization and expense-tracking tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly request that children provide personal information to Gallop & Thrive through an account or server operated by us. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. International processing

Google, Apple, and their service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Their respective privacy policies describe the safeguards they apply to international processing.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

For receipt and profile information stored only on your device, you can exercise control directly in the App by editing or deleting records and profiles, or by deleting the App. For information processed by Apple or Google, use the privacy controls and request mechanisms provided by those companies.

You may contact us about a privacy request using the details below. We may need to verify your request and may be unable to retrieve information that is stored only on your device.
