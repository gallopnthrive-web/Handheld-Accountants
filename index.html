On-device processing and storage Receipt text recognition is performed on the device using Apple Vision technologies. Based on the current version of the App, Gallop & Thrive does not upload your receipt images or extracted receipt contents to a Gallop & Thrive server for text recognition. Profiles, receipt records, saved receipt images, thumbnails, preferences, and Full Version status are stored locally on your device using Apple storage technologies. This locally stored information may also be included in device backups depending on your Apple device and backup settings. If a future version introduces cloud synchronization, remote receipt processing, analytics, or another server-based feature, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when that processing begins. ## 4.
