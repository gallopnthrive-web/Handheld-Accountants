Frequently asked questions

How do I create or switch profiles?

Use the people button at the top left of the workspace to return to Select Profile. Select an existing profile or tap plus to add one. New profile names can contain up to 40 characters. Each profile has its own company, currency and tax settings. The app reopens Home for the last selected profile.

Where do I find the main tools?

Home provides an overview. Receipts lets you search and manage records; Scan adds receipts from the camera or Photos; Finance contains Export Receipts and Spending Summary; More contains profile tools, categories and warranty records. You can also open Settings and the in-app Help for instructions.

How do I scan and check a receipt?

Choose Scan, then Scan with Camera or Choose from Photos. Camera scanning requires a supported physical iPhone or iPad. Review the trading name, legal entity if applicable, date, amount, currency, payment method and other extracted details before saving. Recognition can make mistakes or leave fields for you to enter.

Why is some detected information incorrect?

Blur, shadows, folds, faded text, unusual layouts and receipt language can affect recognition. Keep the full receipt visible on a flat, contrasting surface with good lighting. Correct the details before saving. For a saved record, swipe left on the receipt and choose Edit. The app may recognise a registered company name rather than the shop's trading name; check both fields against the receipt.

Why can I not save an amount?

An empty or invalid amount cannot be saved. Use your device region's decimal separator, and remove letters or unrelated symbols. The amount keyboard has a plus/minus control for negative refunds and a Done button to close it. If an external keyboard or pasted text produces unexpected characters, enter a valid amount before saving.

How are tax amounts calculated?

The app estimates tax from a tax-inclusive total using the single rate configured for the record. It does not choose a tax rate from the currency. Monetary values are rounded to each currency's decimal precision per receipt before being added to export totals; net equals rounded total minus rounded tax. Mixed rates, exemptions and local calculation rules can differ, so check against the original receipt. The app prepares records; it does not file tax returns or certify accounting compliance.

How are different currencies handled?

Receipts keep their recorded currencies. Finance summaries and export review show currency-separated totals instead of converting or combining different currencies. Check the currency when reviewing a scanned receipt; a profile preference is not confirmation of the currency printed on the receipt.

How do I find and organise receipts?

In Receipts, search by trading name, legal entity, notes or receipt number. Use the calendar and filters for dates, categories and Business/Personal expenses. Assign categories while reviewing or editing a receipt. Existing receipts remain Uncategorised until assigned. Spending Summary, custom category management and suggestions based on previous receipts require Full Version; basic built-in categories remain available without it.

How do I track warranty dates?

While reviewing or editing a receipt, enable Track Warranty Expiry and set the expiry date. Open Warranty Records from Home or More to search and filter by warranty status. Archived receipts remain included. The dates are your records, not confirmation that the seller or manufacturer accepts a warranty claim.

How do warranty reminders work?

Reminders are off by default for each profile. Open Warranty Records, then Warranty Reminders, enable notifications and choose 7, 14 or 30 days before expiry. Reminders are scheduled locally for 9 AM; past reminder times are not sent again. Open the app regularly to refresh the limited reminder queue. If permission is denied, check notification settings on your iPhone or iPad. Records still work without notifications. Notification previews may show profile and merchant names; control preview visibility in system Settings.

How do I export receipts?

Choose Export Receipts in Finance or in the Receipts menu. In Prepare Export, select record status, dates, category, Business/Personal, currency and other filters, then select Review Export. The Receipts menu starts with the currently filtered list. Review the records, warnings and currency-separated totals, then choose Export CSV, Financial Summary PDF or Receipt PDF Archive with images. Preparation and review do not require Full Version; creating files does.

Does exporting archive or delete my receipts?

CSV and financial summary PDF exports do not archive receipts. After a receipt PDF is successfully shared or saved, a separate confirmation lets you choose whether to archive the exported active records. Cancelling sharing or declining archiving leaves them unchanged. Archiving hides records from the active list; it does not delete them or end their warranty tracking. Deletion is a separate, permanent action.

Why does CSV use English headers, or show an apostrophe?

CSV uses fixed English column headers, YYYY-MM-DD dates, decimal points and UTF-8 encoding for consistent import. Text that could be interpreted as a spreadsheet formula is protected with an initial apostrophe; numeric refunds stay negative numbers. When importing, set receipt-number and tax-ID columns to text if necessary to preserve leading zeros, and check the receiving software's field mapping.

How do I restore Full Version?

Open More, choose Upgrade to Full Version, then Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Check your internet connection if the entitlement is not recognised. If it still fails, contact support with the app version and error message. Do not send your Apple Account password or payment-card details.

Where is my data stored, and are exports a backup?

Receipt recognition uses Apple's on-device technology. Profiles, receipt records and saved images are stored locally; no app account or sign-in is required. The app does not provide its own cloud receipt synchronisation. Deleting a receipt or profile permanently removes associated records and images. CSV and PDF preserve exported information but cannot fully restore the app's database. Keep an appropriate device backup, especially before installing a beta. Files you save or share elsewhere must be managed separately; support cannot remotely recover receipts stored only on your device.

Why do I see ads, or no ads?

The free version can display banner ads. Their availability depends on consent choices, the connection and ad availability. App-open ads are disabled in this updated version. When Full Version is recognised, the app disables its advertisements. If available, Privacy and cookie settings in Settings lets you review advertising privacy choices.

Before contacting us

Try closing and reopening the app. Check camera permission if scanning is unavailable, notification permission for warranty reminders, and the internet connection for purchase restoration or sharing services. Use the system photo picker to select an image; importing a selected photo does not require unrestricted access to the entire photo library. Do not delete and reinstall the app as a troubleshooting step without a recoverable backup: local receipt data may be lost.
